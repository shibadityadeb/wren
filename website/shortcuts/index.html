Anywhere on your Mac

These work in any app while Wren is running, even with its window closed.

KeysWhat it does
Hold ⌥ SpaceTalk to Wren. Hold, speak, let go. Wren transcribes on your Mac and runs it
⌥ ⇧ SpaceType to Wren. Opens the capture panel

Change or turn them off: push-to-talk in Connectors › Voice, the capture shortcut in Settings. If another app already uses a combination, Wren tells you.

Wren's menus

KeysWhat it does
⌘ NNew capture
⌘ 0Open Wren's window
⌘ ,Settings
⌘ /Keyboard shortcuts
⌘ WClose the window (Wren keeps running in the menu bar)
⌘ HHide Wren
⌘ QQuit Wren: stops recording and email checks. Scheduled reminders still arrive
⌘ ?Help: the guide

In the capture panel

KeyWhat it does
ReturnRun what's in the field: save it, set the reminder, ask. After a result: Done
EscCancel a recording, or close the panel
Voice buttonRecord with a click instead of holding the keys. Pause or click Stop to finish
Record againThrow away a misheard sentence and say it again

Clicking away keeps what you typed as a draft.

Things to say or type

Hold ⌥ Space and say them, or press ⌥ ⇧ Space and type them.

Reminders and tasks

  • remind me to call Sam tomorrow at 10 AM
  • remind me to stretch every weekday at 3 PM
  • I need to finish the report by Friday (a deadline, a heads-up the day before and a reminder on the day)
  • remind me one day before the deadline
  • move that reminder to Sunday · snooze the current reminder for 30 minutes · mark the report as done
  • note: Friday is a holiday (just saves it)

Your own items

  • what should I work on today? · what's due this week? · what am I waiting for?

ChatGPT

  • ask ChatGPT what gradient descent is · ask GPT to research the history of the transistor
  • continue that research in ChatGPT

Email and calendar

  • do I have an important email from the recruiter? · open the latest email from Sam · what meetings do I have tomorrow?
