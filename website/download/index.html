Requirements

  • macOS 14 Sonoma or later, on Apple Silicon or Intel.
  • ChatGPT features need a ChatGPT Plus or Pro plan. Local AI is optional and uses Ollama.

Install

  1. Open the DMG and drag Wren into Applications.
  2. Open Wren. This preview isn't notarized by Apple yet, so macOS blocks it the first time.
  3. Go to System Settings › Privacy & Security, find the message about Wren and click Open Anyway.
  4. Optional: check the download with shasum -a 256 -c Wren-macOS-universal-unsigned.dmg.sha256 in the folder that holds both files.

Wren is a preview built by one developer. Your data stays on your Mac; the Privacy Policy explains exactly what leaves it and when.
