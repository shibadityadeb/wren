# Wren shortcuts and commands

A one-page cheat sheet, also at [wrenai.vercel.app/shortcuts](https://wrenai.vercel.app/shortcuts/) and in the app (⌘/). Everything here works with Wren running, including with its window closed (it lives in the menu bar). The details are in the [user guide](USER_GUIDE.md).

## Anywhere on your Mac

| Shortcut | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Hold ⌥ Space** | **Talk to Wren.** Hold, speak, let go. Wren transcribes on your Mac and runs it. Letting go of either key ends the recording |
| **⌥⇧ Space** | **Type to Wren.** Opens the capture panel |

Change or turn off either one: push-to-talk in **Connectors › Voice**, typing in **Settings**. If another app already uses a combination, Wren says so and you can pick another.

## In the capture panel

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Return** | Run what's in the field (save, set the reminder, ask…). After a result: **Done** (closes; doesn't run it again) |
| **Esc** | Cancel a recording, or close the panel |
| **Voice** button | Record with a click instead of holding the keys. Pause or click **Stop** to finish |
| **Record again** | Throw away a misheard transcript and say it again |

Clicking away keeps what you typed as a draft.

## Menus

| Shortcut | What it does |
|---|---|
| **⌘N** | New capture |
| **⌘0** | Open Wren's window |
| **⌘,** | Settings |
| **⌘W** | Close the window (Wren keeps running in the menu bar) |
| **⌘Q** | Quit Wren (stops any recording and email checks; scheduled reminders still arrive) |
| **⌘/** | Keyboard shortcuts (Help › Keyboard Shortcuts, also Settings › Keyboard shortcuts…) |
| **⌘?** | Help: the step-by-step guide on the website |
| **⌘1 … ⌘9** | Go to Overview, Items, Inbox, Goals, Review, Delegate, Connectors, Local AI, Settings |
| Right-click the menu-bar bird | Open Wren, New Capture, Voice Command, Settings, Quit |

## Things to say or type

Say them while holding ⌥ Space, or type them after ⌥⇧ Space.

**Reminders and tasks**
- `remind me to call Sam tomorrow at 10 AM` (or `can you make a reminder to…`, `don't let me forget to…`)
- `remind me to stretch every weekday at 3 PM`
- `remind me to ask GPT about taxes tomorrow` (a reminder for later; nothing goes to ChatGPT now)
- `I need to finish the report by Friday` (a task with a deadline, a heads-up the day before and a reminder on the day)
- `make a task to follow up with the recruiter` (a task with no deadline)
- `remind me one day before the deadline` (for the task you just made; shows a preview)
- `move that reminder to Sunday` / `snooze the current reminder for 30 minutes` (shows a preview; Return confirms)
- `mark the report as done`
- `note: Friday is a holiday` (just saves the text)

**Your own items**
- `what should I work on today?`
- `what am I waiting for?`
- `what's due this week?` / `what deadlines are approaching?`
- `which goals have I made no progress on?`

**ChatGPT** (naming GPT or ChatGPT always means ChatGPT)
- `ask ChatGPT what gradient descent is`: opens the ChatGPT website with your request, ready to send
- `ask GPT to research the history of the transistor`
- `ask GPT to think deeply about whether I should learn Rust or Go first`
- `continue that research in ChatGPT` / `open that in GPT` / `find more sources`

**Local AI** (no assistant named)
- `what is gradient descent?` / `explain this error`

**Email and calendar**
- `do I have an important email from the recruiter?`
- `open the latest email from Sam`
- `what meetings do I have tomorrow?`

If Wren isn't sure what you meant, it asks one short question; answer it the same way.

## Notification buttons

| Notification | Buttons |
|---|---|
| Reminder | **Done**, **Remind me in 1 hour**, **Remind me at…** (type `9pm`, `tomorrow 10am`) |
| Day-before heads-up for a deadline | **Done** |
| GPT finished (run in Wren) | **View result** |
| Important email | Click: **opens it in Gmail**. Buttons: **Open in Gmail**, **Show in Wren**, **Snooze alert for 1 hour**, **Mark as read** (if allowed), **Dismiss alert** |
| Email digest | **Open digest** |

Clicking a notification itself opens exactly what it's about.
